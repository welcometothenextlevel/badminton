A scoreboard without the pennant paperwork. Graded, mixed and rotating draws for players who want the game to mean something.

How it works

  1. 01

    Tell us roughly where you sit

    Beginner, social, or competitive. If you get it wrong we move you — grading settles itself within a couple of weeks.

  2. 02

    Come as a pair, or on your own

    No partner is not a problem. Rotating draws pair you with someone different each round, which is how most people end up with a regular partner anyway.

  3. 03

    Play, then have a drink about it

    Short matches, quick turnaround, everyone gets a decent number of games. Results tracked across the season without anyone chasing you for a form.

The formats

Graded doubles

Fixed pairs in a grade that suits you, playing a round each week. The closest thing to pennant without the commitment.

Mixed

Mixed doubles draws, graded the same way. Popular with couples, housemates and anyone who has been dragged along and then got hooked.

Rotating social

Turn up alone, get drawn with a different partner each round. The easiest way into the centre if you do not know anyone yet.

League nights

For players who want more structure, weeknight ladders run across multiple grades with results tracked through the season and promotion between divisions. Same courts, same lighting, a bit more on the line.
