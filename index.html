About the centre

For players,
by players

Everest Badminton Centre started with the vision to provide the northern suburbs, which has plenty of people who love badminton, with a premium place to play and spend quality time together. Not multi-sport facilities, not school halls, not venues where the lights are in your eyes on every clear.

From the playing surface to the ceiling height, the background colour, the seating areas and the lighting, everything has been designed by people who play the game several nights a week and who know exactly what makes for a great session. Twelve courts, no compromises, and a pro shop where you can buy or rent equipment and shuttles.

Bring your kids, bring your parents, bring the mate who hasn't picked up a racquet since school. First-timers and pennant players share the same floor here.

  • The northern suburbs' premier badminton centre
  • Family friendly, from first hit to seasoned athlete
  • Minutes from the Hume Highway and Western Ring Road

Why Everest

The name isn't decoration. Everest is the benchmark we measured the build against — the surface, the lighting, the spec — and it's the shorthand for whatever it is you are chasing. Every player has a summit in mind. We built the mountain worth climbing.

Facilities

Specified properly,
from the floor up

Twelve BWF-standard courts, purpose-built with exacting attention to the things that matter. Here's what that actually means when you're on court.

12 world class courts

BWF standard, full-size.

High impact absorption

A sprung, shock-absorbing court system that takes the load off knees, ankles and hips. Less soreness the next morning, more seasons in your legs.

Anti-glare honeycomb lighting

Premium, honeycomb LED lighting precisely angled so the shuttle stays visible at the top of its arc and never disappears into a hotspot.

Easy access and parking

Ample on-site parking and a location built for industry traffic, sitting right next to the arterial roads. Turn up ten minutes before your booking, not forty.

Amenities

Clean showers and storage, plus a pro shop with racquets and equipment to buy or rent, and shuttles at the desk when yours have had enough.

Spectator seating

Seating areas for parents, partners and teammates, comfortable enough that they'll actually stay for the third game.

Everyone has an Everest to conquer.

Yours might be a first competitive game, a stubborn backhand, or simply getting back on court after ten years off. The altitude's the same — we just built somewhere worth climbing.

Programs

Whatever you
turn up for

Casual hitters, competitive squads, school-holiday juniors and Friday-night work teams. Six routes up, one mountain. Bookings open ahead of launch, register your interest and we'll get in touch.

  1. 01

    Casual court hire

    Book by the half-hour, on the day or a fortnight out. Off-peak rates through the weekday, extended evenings until 11pm for anyone who finishes work late.

  2. 02

    Coaching for beginners

    Small-group sessions that start at grip and footwork rather than assuming you already own a racquet. Rental gear available while you work out whether it sticks.

  3. 03

    Junior programs

    Age-grouped squads after school and through the holidays, built around movement and game sense before technique drilling. Parents can watch from the seating, not the doorway.

  4. 04

    Social competitions

    Graded, mixed and rotating draws for players who want a scoreboard without the pennant paperwork. Turn up as a pair or let us find you a partner.

  5. 05

    League nights

    Structured weeknight ladders across multiple grades, with results tracked across the season and promotion between divisions.

  6. 06

    Private and corporate bookings

    Take a block of courts for a club training night, a birthday, a school group or a work team. We'll sort equipment, grading and the run sheet.
