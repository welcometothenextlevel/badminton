About the centre

Built by players,
run by players

Everest Badminton Centre started with a simple gripe: the northern suburbs had plenty of people who loved badminton and nowhere decent to play it. Sticky floors, borrowed school halls, lights in your eyes on every clear. So we're building the alternative ourselves.

Every call — the surface, the ceiling height, the run-off behind the baseline, the spacing between courts — has been made by people who play the game several nights a week and know exactly what ruins a session. Twelve courts, no compromises, and a front desk that knows the difference between a feather and a nylon shuttle.

It's a serious venue that isn't precious about it. Bring your kids, bring your parents, bring the mate who hasn't picked up a racquet since school. First-timers and pennant players share the same floor here.

  • The northern suburbs' premier badminton hub
  • Family friendly, from first hit to seasoned athlete
  • Minutes from the Hume Highway and Western Ring Road

Facilities

Specified properly,
from the floor up

Twelve match-standard courts, purpose-built rather than converted. Here's what that actually means when you're on court.

12 world-class courts

Full-size, correctly spaced and with proper run-off at the back — so a deep clear doesn't end with you in the next rally.

High impact absorption

A sprung, shock-absorbing court system that takes the load off knees, ankles and hips. Less soreness the next morning, more seasons in your legs.

Glare-free lighting

Even, high-level LED lighting aimed so the shuttle stays visible at the top of its arc and never disappears into a hotspot.

Easy access and parking

Free on-site parking and a location built for industry traffic, sitting right next to the arterial roads. Turn up ten minutes before your booking, not forty.

Change rooms and amenities

Clean change rooms, showers and secure storage, plus a restring service and shuttles at the desk when yours have had enough.

Spectator seating

Proper viewing areas for parents, partners and teammates — comfortable enough that they'll actually stay for the third game.

Everyone has an Everest to conquer.

Yours might be a first competitive game, a stubborn backhand, or simply getting back on court after ten years off. The altitude's the same — we just built somewhere worth climbing.

Programs

Whatever you
turn up for

Casual hitters, competitive squads, school-holiday juniors and Friday-night work teams. Bookings open ahead of launch — register your interest and we'll get in touch.

  1. 01

    Casual court hire

    Book by the hour, on the day or a fortnight out. Off-peak rates through the day, extended evenings until 11pm for anyone who finishes work late.

  2. 02

    Coaching for beginners

    Small-group sessions that start at grip and footwork rather than assuming you already own a racquet. Loan gear available while you work out whether it sticks.

  3. 03

    Junior programs

    Age-grouped squads after school and through the holidays, built around movement and game sense before technique drilling. Parents can watch from the seating, not the doorway.

  4. 04

    Social competitions

    Graded, mixed and rotating draws for players who want a scoreboard without the pennant paperwork. Turn up as a pair or let us find you a partner.

  5. 05

    League nights

    Structured weeknight ladders across multiple grades, with results tracked across the season and promotion between divisions.

  6. 06

    Private and corporate bookings

    Take a block of courts for a club training night, a birthday, a school group or a work team. We'll sort equipment, grading and the run sheet.
