Membership

Memberships

For players who are in the building more than once a week. Founding membership is $60 for your first month, and it comes with a few things standard membership does not.

Register your interest Call 0426 620 274

You never need a membership to play

Worth saying first: there is no membership wall. Anyone can book a court, join a social draw or put their kid in a junior squad without joining anything. Membership exists for people who play often enough that it starts paying for itself.

The founding member offer

Founding membership is $60 for your first month. It is the one genuine advantage of joining before the doors open rather than after.

First month only

Founding membership

$60 for your first month

$60/first month

  • A custom EBC ambassador t shirt
  • Your $60 can go towards court bookings if used before the end of December
  • $5 off both peak and off-peak court hire
  • 5% off pro shop purchases

Standard membership

Ongoing, after the founding month

  • $5 off both peak and off-peak court hire
  • 5% off pro shop purchases
  • No ambassador t shirt
  • Membership fee is not redeemable against court bookings
Register interest

The $60 founding rate applies to your first month only. The t shirt and the redeemable fee are what separate it from standard membership.

Court rates

Membership is not a wall. Anyone can book a court at these rates, and members take $5 off both. All prices in AUD and per court, not per player, with up to four players included.

  • Peak, $32 an hour, weeknights 4:00pm to 11:00pm plus weekends and public holidays
  • Off-peak, $27 an hour, Monday to Friday 7:00am to 4:00pm
