Programs

Corporate &
private hire

Take a block of courts for a work team, a birthday, a school group or a club training night. We'll sort the equipment, the grading and the run sheet.

Register your interest Call 0426 620 274

What we host

Work teams and corporate nights

Twelve courts under one roof means you can bring a whole department and nobody sits out. We grade the draw so the person who played at university and the person who has never held a racquet both have a decent night.

Birthdays and private groups

Court blocks for parties, family gatherings and groups of mates. Racquets and shuttles included, and the pro shop is there if someone forgets their shoes.

School and community groups

Weekday daytime is the quietest and cheapest time in the building, which suits school groups and community programs. Proper flooring and glare-free lighting make a real difference with large groups of beginners.

Club training nights

Existing clubs can take a standing block of courts each week. Talk to us about the nights you want before the timetable fills.

What's included

  • Your block of courts, set up before you arrive
  • Racquets and shuttles for everyone playing
  • A graded draw or round-robin, run by us if you want it
  • Spectator seating for anyone not on court
  • Ample on-site parking, right off the arterial roads

Getting it organised

Tell us the date, roughly how many people, and whether you want it run for you or just want the courts. We'll come back with what fits. Larger blocks are worth locking in early — weeknight evenings go first.
